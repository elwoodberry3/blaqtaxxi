## Assessment
This isn't an Uber clone. Uber solves dispatch across many drivers. BLAQTAXXI has one driver and one fare at a time, so the whole product is a **travel-time-aware appointment scheduler with a live ETA layer**. That is a much smaller build.

**Core rule.** A new booking is only offered if both of these hold:
- The driver can reach the new pickup on time from the previous drop-off, including a buffer.
- The driver can reach the next booked pickup on time from the new drop-off.

That rule is what "appointments have to account for where he's coming from" means in code.

**Rider states:** Booked, Queued (with a countdown, e.g. "pickup in 32 hrs"), Driver location revealed (T-30 min), En route, Arrived, Complete.

**Driver view:**
- Day timeline.
- One-tap status buttons.
- A warning when a running-late ride threatens the next pickup.
- An auto-text to the affected rider.

**Pricing from your transcript:**
- $20 flat in DFW.
- $25–30 when the trip runs longer than 30 minutes.
- "Louisville, Lil' M, $25" looks like a transcription error, so I haven't built any rule from it.

## Risks
- **Live location on a PWA.** iOS won't reliably track in the background. Location updates only while the driver's app is open, or on each status tap, which matches your "dropping his location" idea. Truly continuous tracking would need a native app.
- **Travel-time accuracy.** Feasibility depends on a routing API (Google Routes or Mapbox), which costs per call and is wrong in DFW traffic. A buffer setting is essential.
- **Regulatory and insurance.** I don't know whether a single driver taking app bookings counts as a Texas rideshare operator. Personal auto policies also commonly exclude paid rides, and DFW Airport pickups typically need a permit. Verify all three before launch. I'm not a lawyer.
- **Payments.** Card fees take a bigger share of a $20 fare than of larger tickets. I'd verify the current Stripe rate. Cash App and Zelle are also common with riders, so decide whether to accept them.

## Opportunity (One Build, Three Doors)
