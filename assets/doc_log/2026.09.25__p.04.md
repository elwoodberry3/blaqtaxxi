## Layout system review
I copied it in unmodified, verified with `diff`. It has good bones: no raw hex, a manifest-first workflow, and clear product guardrails. It also has real problems, recorded in `docs/LAYOUT_AMENDMENTS.md`:

- **Contrast failures.**
  - The gray text is 3.19:1 on white and 2.95:1 on the canvas color, and AA needs 4.5:1.
  - The green success pill is 4.02:1.
  - I proposed a darker gray for text (`#686D71`, 5.23:1 on white), which needs the client's approval.
- **Rider-account screens.** The bottom nav, profile, trip history and saved places all assume a logged-in rider. Your customer is link-based, so I'm not using them on customer surfaces.
- **Coverage gaps.** It has six customer layouts. It has no slot picker, checkout, driver console or admin backend. `docs/SCREEN_MAP.md` lists about 40 screens, and Phase 0.5 wireframes the missing ones.
- **Missing commands.** `SKILL.md` describes `/wireframe` and `/brand-audit`, but they don't exist as commands. I added them.
- **Other gaps.** There is no warning color for "running late", because red is reserved for destructive actions. The palette's source names also look like a football team's, so I noted not to use team names, logos or imagery.
